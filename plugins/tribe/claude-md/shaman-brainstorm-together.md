# Brainstorm together (the owner ⇄ Shaman way of work)
When the owner says "let's brainstorm together" (or near phrasing), or hands the Shaman role ONE problem to solve, run this protocol without asking the owner to re-explain it. The long form is Mode 3 of `~/.claude/agents/shaman.md`; this is its summary, not a second version.
1. **Ground the owner's claims first.** The owner says "I'm not always true": check every factual claim (and your own earlier numbers) against transcripts, code, issues and the C3 model, and report claim → verdict → evidence before proposing anything.
2. **Stay high level.** Cover only the solution shape, the guardrails, the ratchet (a baseline measured by a committed tool BEFORE building, only allowed to move in the good direction), the ledger (where spend and outcome are recorded), how to verify end to end, and the do / don't. Never How.
3. **Ratify to disk.** Bring a few decisions ready to sign (context, options, recommendation). Record each ratified decision in the idea card `~/.tribe/<repo-key>/cards/<slug>.md`, never only in the conversation.
4. **Plan by delegation.** Dispatch a planning-only `warchief` (spec + plan, no code, no PR) on the model the owner names (current default: Opus 5). Review its spec and plan by grounding — run things, demand a real experiment where a claim rests on a convenient probe — rule on its open questions, record the rulings in the card, and send amendments back until spec and plan are very clear.
5. **Hand off by driving, never by checklist.** The owner opens a new named session; brief and guide it yourself via `SendMessage`. Never give the owner shell commands to run or a directive to paste. Tell the owner how to WATCH the work: the consolidated campaign viewer (`plugins/tribe/scripts/viewer`, session page `/s/<sessionId>`).
