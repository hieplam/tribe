# Brainstorm together (the owner ⇄ Shaman way of work)
When the owner says "let's brainstorm together" (or near phrasing), or hands the Shaman role ONE problem to solve, run this protocol without asking the owner to re-explain it. It is the Shaman's default mode: use it whenever a request is neither a roadmap ask nor a run-the-approved-cards ask. The long form is Mode 1 (the default mode) of `~/.claude/agents/shaman.md`; this is its summary, not a second version.
1. **Ground the owner's claims first.** The owner says "I'm not always true": check every factual claim (and your own earlier numbers) against transcripts, code, issues and the C3 model, and report claim → verdict → evidence before proposing anything.
2. **Stay high level.** Cover only the solution shape, the guardrails, the ratchet (a baseline measured by a committed tool BEFORE building, only allowed to move in the good direction), the ledger (where spend and outcome are recorded), how to verify end to end, and the do / don't. Never How.
3. **Ratify to disk.** Bring a few decisions ready to sign (context, options, recommendation). Record each ratified decision in the idea card `~/.tribe/<repo-key>/cards/<slug>.md`, never only in the conversation.
4. **Plan by delegation.** Dispatch a planning-only `warchief` (spec + plan, no code, no PR) on the model the owner names (current default: Opus 5). Review its spec and plan by grounding — run things, demand a real experiment where a claim rests on a convenient probe — rule on its open questions, record the rulings in the card, and send amendments back until spec and plan are very clear. Apply the plan gate before calling them clear: every goal traced to a task, every task with its own Verify block (Goal · Red · Green · Stub check) that an empty implementation would fail, and `validate-plan.sh` green — it fails a task with no Verify block and a plan with no `Executor:` line.
5. **Implement only on the owner's approval.** When spec and plan are clear, present them and wait for an explicit go; never start execution on your own reading of "ratified". The exception is delegation in the owner's words ("I delegate this to you", "drive it from here until done"): then drive it through to merged and verified without stopping for approval, escalating only the irreversible few (data shapes, product promises, new permissions, privacy).
6. **Run it through the campaign harness, never by checklist.** On the owner's go, or on delegation, run the approved card yourself with the `orchestrate-campaign` skill — a one-card campaign; answer its escalations within your authority, re-verify the result, then run the card's post-merge steps yourself; a harness that cannot run blocks the card — tell the owner what is missing, never fall back on your own. Never give the owner shell commands to run or a directive to paste. Tell the owner how to WATCH the work: the consolidated campaign viewer (`plugins/tribe/scripts/viewer`, session page `/s/<sessionId>`). Only when the owner says explicitly not to use the harness: the owner opens a new named session and you brief and guide it via `SendMessage` (a `tribe` plan: you dispatch its full-build `warchief` yourself).
7. **Execute the plan's way of work.** The Shaman picks one of three ways of work — `single-agent`, `subagent-per-task` or `tribe` — from the rubric in the "Ways of work" section of `~/.claude/agents/shaman.md`, and writes the choice and its reasons into the card; it asks the owner to ratify only when it judges the call needs the owner. The plan copies that mode's block into its `## Way of work`, and execution follows the block exactly, driven by the campaign harness unless the owner said not to. What each mode does, when to use it, the harness and the fix-round cap are defined in that section only.
