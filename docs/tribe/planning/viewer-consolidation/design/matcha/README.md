# Matcha

Whisked green tea on cream paper: a warm off-white page, a pale foam panel, and deep pine ink that reads like a printed notebook rather than a screen. It is the softest of the three — rounded corners, a foam wash behind every label — and it wants you to sit with a long transcript rather than scan one.

**Accent** — a deep matcha green (`#2c6344` light, `#8fc79c` dark), chosen because the app's one loud signal is already green: the live dot. Making the accent the same family means "this session is alive" and "this is the thing you clicked" rhyme instead of arguing, and the green stays legible as text at 6.30:1.

**Bad at** — green is the status colour in every developer tool, so a green accent slightly blunts the live dot's urgency at a glance; the warm cream paper also throws a faint yellow cast over screenshots and pasted images, and the foam washes give it the least crisp edges of the three if you like your tools sharp.

**Measured** — body ink on paper: 13.39:1 light, 15.35:1 dark. Every text token clears 4.5:1 in both modes.
Open `preview.html` in any browser (no server needed) and use the toggle at the top right to force light or dark.
