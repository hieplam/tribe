# Sea salt

Wet slate and sea mist: a cool grey-blue page, a salt-flat panel, and a near-black slate for type. It is the crispest and coolest of the three — 2px corners, hairline rules, a small accent tick before each label — and it is the one that most disappears behind the content.

**Accent** — a deep harbour blue (`#1f5a77` light, `#82c2dd` dark), chosen as the honest contrast candidate: cool where the other two are warm, and the one accent that reads unambiguously as "interactive" to anyone who has used a browser, without borrowing a framework's default blue.

**Bad at** — it is the least "chill" of the three against the owner's brief; a cool grey-blue is what most developer tools already look like, so it has the weakest personality and the highest chance of feeling generic. The cool paper also makes long monospace passages feel slightly clinical next to matcha's cream.

**Measured** — body ink on paper: 14.41:1 light, 15.57:1 dark. Every text token clears 4.5:1 in both modes.
Open `preview.html` in any browser (no server needed) and use the toggle at the top right to force light or dark.
